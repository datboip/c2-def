HOW TO PLAY

The world is a cube — each face a biome, gravity at the core. Creeps march to your single crystal. In CLASSIC there is ONE portal — wall and dig so they travel the longest possible way around the cube. In SIEGE every biome that wakes opens its own portal (multiplayer is always siege — each player owns one).

WAKING THE CUBE — biomes wake on a schedule, or breach early: click the countdown chip (costs gold, grants a cache + that biome's native tower). Native towers hit +25% at home: cannon·meadow, tesla·forest, sniper·desert, frost·tundra, mortar·volcanic, laser·caverns.

YOUR HERO is always fielded — it sentries when unmanned, levels up from kills, V possesses it, click it for the skill tree, the pilot ability gives it AI.

SCORE: +5/kill · +100+10w per wave (+50 perfect) · +150/biome · −25/leak · −400 overrun.
GUNS, NOT WALLS — towers never bend the route; only terrain does. Towers standing on the route get trampled.

MATTER IS CONSERVED — dig +1 dirt, place −1. Mining is a gamble: 3g a roll, 60% +5%/level; your first 10 digs are free and guaranteed. Layers drop gold → iron → shards, bedrock at 3. Press K for the WORKSHOP: picks gate harder biomes (iron pick → frozen tundra, crystal pick → volcanic/caverns), the bucket moves liquids, the shovel and prospector lens sweeten mining.

WATER slows creeps to 45% (they prefer dry routes) · ice speeds them up · lava burns. Place dirt on water to reclaim it as land.

PATH DOTS: green full pace · yellow slowed · red crawling — reroute them through red. 1-deep pits slow walkers, 2-deep stop them. The route can never be fully sealed.
