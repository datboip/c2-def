HOW TO PLAY · C² DEFENSE

The world is a cube; each face is a biome, gravity points at the core. Creeps pour from portals — one per awake biome, tinted to match it — and all march to your single crystal. One way in per biome, one way out.

WAKING THE CUBE — you start on the meadow. Biomes wake in order: forest → desert → tundra → volcanic → caverns, on a schedule set by difficulty — or breach early by clicking the countdown chip (costs gold, comes with an expedition cache + the biome's native tower). Every portal sits at its face's far edge: the invasion marches the full face, over the rim, and onward — one continuous front.

NATIVE TOWERS — cannon starts with you (meadow). tesla→forest · sniper→desert · frost→tundra · mortar→volcanic · laser→caverns. A tower on its home biome hits +25%.

HAZARDS — forest undergrowth slows creeps · desert quicksand makes pits lethal-slow · tundra ice speeds them up but frost slows stick longer · volcanic embers and cavern static burn HP over time.

KNOW YOUR ENEMY — grunt basic fodder · runner fast but fragile, slows wreck it · tank 2.8× hp, nearly stops on slopes — snipe it · jumper (w6+) hops 1-high walls, 2-high stops it · crawler (w7+) ignores ALL terrain — raw dps only · healer (w6+) pulses heals, kill it first · shield (w9+) 35% armor, big single hits beat it · boss (every 5th) regenerates — focus fire, freeze, burn.

PATH DOTS read the route's speed: green full pace · yellow slowed · red crawling. Reroute them through red.

MATTER IS CONSERVED — dig +1 dirt, place −1, nothing from nowhere (the AI obeys too). Walkers climb 1 (slow), jumpers hop walls, crawlers scale anything.

GUNS, NOT WALLS — towers never block the route. Only terrain bends it: dig trenches, raise walls, reroute through your kill zones. A tower standing on the route gets trampled (towers have HP) — building in the highway is a greedy gamble. The route can never be fully sealed.

YOUR HERO is always on the field: unmanned it sentries (auto-shoots nearby creeps); V flies you into it wherever it stands; the pilot ability (2 shards, once) gives it AI — it roams the cube hunting creeps on its own. Dig and wall tools live in the build bar too, so you can terraform from god view.
